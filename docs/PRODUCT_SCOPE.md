# Product scope — October 2, 2026

The user's latest instruction supersedes the original enterprise product contract. This product helps companies retain people by transferring them to another internal team that needs their skills, location and salary profile.

The main journeys are:

1. **Teams needing people:** specify the reason, role, skills, location/work mode, FTE, needed dates and optional annual base salary budget/currency.
2. **People needing a role:** HR or a manager can flag a person using **Consider for internal roles**, including people who have not requested or do not know they need another role. The flag controls matching eligibility; it does not change employment. Capture the reason, availability date, optional placement deadline, location/work mode, relocation preference and available FTE. Skills belong to the person's profile. Changes are dated, audited and restricted to authorized HR organization scope or manager reporting scope.
3. **Find matches:** show the evidence and gaps for skills, location, timing, FTE and authorized salary comparisons. Missing or restricted evidence is unknown. A match never automatically changes employment or selects someone for redundancy.
4. **Transfers:** reviewers propose and approve an internal move, then explicitly commit it with effective dates and a history. Permanent transfers must not silently use temporary loan semantics.
5. **Supporting data and access:** people, teams, roles, skills, source imports, sign-in and scoped permissions support these journeys.

Salary stays in separately protected compensation records. Planning access does not grant salary or identity access. Hidden salary must not alter candidate visibility or ordering. Reasons should describe the placement need, not contain unnecessary sensitive personal information. All records remain tenant scoped, dated and audited.

Finance forecasts, scenarios, position/seat planning, hiring pipelines, broad reporting catalogs, legal-hold workflows and general HRIS administration are outside the active product roadmap. Existing modules and records remain for compatibility; hiding them from navigation is not a claim that their implementation or data has been removed.

Release completion requires the above journeys to work together and current-source verification. Passing checks for legacy modules does not establish completion of this product.
